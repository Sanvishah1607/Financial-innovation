## ⚠️ Important FinTech Hackathon Rules
- **Rule 1**: Never ask for real bank passwords, UPI PINs, or CVVs.
- **Rule 2**: Keep rule-based algorithms transparent; do not claim black-box AI or real credit scores.
- **Rule 3**: Test every feature with edge cases before marking it as completed.
